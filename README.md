# 🛍️ E-Commerce Customer Analytics

> An end-to-end customer analytics project focused on understanding customer behavior, segmentation, customer value, and revenue concentration using transactional data.

## 🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw transaction records do not clearly reveal which customers are most valuable, which customers are at risk, or where revenue is concentrated.

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior to identify **high-value, loyal, at-risk, and high-potential customers** and provide actionable recommendations for improving retention and revenue.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Identify repeat and one-time customers
- Segment customers using RFM analysis
- Estimate Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Identify high-value customers
- Analyze revenue concentration using Pareto analysis
- Identify high-potential customer groups
- Develop customer retention strategies
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the **Online Retail** transactional dataset containing e-commerce purchase records.

The cleaned transaction dataset contains:

- **392,693 transactions**
- **4,338 unique customers**
- Transaction dates
- Products
- Quantities
- Unit prices
- Customer IDs
- Countries

## 📈 Customer Behavior Analysis

The analysis examined:

- Customer purchasing frequency
- Customer spending behavior
- Repeat vs. one-time customers
- Average customer revenue
- Customer purchasing patterns

### Key Finding

**65.58% of customers were repeat buyers**, while **34.42% were one-time customers**.

## 🎯 RFM Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented using:

- **Recency (R)** — How recently customers purchased
- **Frequency (F)** — How often customers purchased
- **Monetary (M)** — How much customers spent

### Customer Segments

- 🏆 Champions
- 💎 Loyal Customers
- 🚀 Potential Loyalists
- 🆕 New Customers
- ⚠️ At-Risk Customers
- 💤 Hibernating Customers
- 🛒 Occasional Customers
- 👥 Other Customers

## 💰 Customer Value Analysis

### 💵 Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Estimated CLV was used to identify customers with stronger long-term revenue potential.

### 📈 Pareto Analysis

The analysis examined revenue concentration across customers to determine how much revenue was generated by the highest-value portion of the customer base.

### 🏆 High-Value Customers

The analysis identified the customers generating the highest total revenue for targeted relationship and retention strategies.

### 💰 Revenue Contribution

The **Champions segment contained 957 customers and generated 65.17% of total revenue**, making this the most valuable customer segment.

### 🚀 High-Potential Customers

Potential Loyalists and other promising customer groups were identified as opportunities for targeted engagement and conversion into higher-value customers.

## 💡 Executive Business Recommendations

### 🏆 Protect Champions

Prioritize retention of Champions because they generate the largest share of revenue.

### 💎 Strengthen Loyal Customers

Use personalized offers and loyalty programs to increase their long-term value.

### 🚀 Convert Potential Loyalists

Use targeted promotions and personalized recommendations to encourage more frequent purchases.

### ⚠️ Retain At-Risk Customers

Develop re-engagement campaigns to prevent valuable customers from becoming inactive.

### 💤 Reactivate Hibernating Customers

Use targeted win-back campaigns and incentives to encourage inactive customers to return.

### 🆕 Nurture New Customers

Create onboarding and follow-up campaigns to encourage first-time buyers to become repeat customers.
