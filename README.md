# E-Commerce Customer Analytics

## 📋 Project Overview

An end-to-end customer analytics project using e-commerce transaction data to understand **customer behavior, segmentation, customer value, and revenue concentration**.

The project applies **RFM Analysis, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), and Pareto Analysis** to identify valuable, loyal, at-risk, and high-potential customers and generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The project addresses key business questions:

- Who are the repeat and one-time customers?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- Which customer segments are most valuable?
- Which customers are at risk?
- Which customers have growth potential?
- How concentrated is revenue across customers?

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Cleaning

Key data preparation steps included:

- Removing duplicates and invalid transactions
- Handling missing customer information
- Removing cancelled and non-product transactions
- Standardizing date fields
- Validating the cleaned dataset

**Final dataset:** 392,693 transactions and 4,338 customers.

## ⚙️ Feature Engineering

Created customer and transaction-level features including:

- Revenue
- Invoice Year, Month, Quarter, Day and Weekday
- Customer Tenure
- Purchase Frequency
- Average Order Value
- Revenue Band
- Customer Type

## 👥 Customer Dataset

Transactions were aggregated into a customer-level dataset containing:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- First & Last Purchase
- Average Order Value
- Customer Tenure
- Purchase Frequency
- Recency
- RFM Scores
- Estimated CLV

## 📈 Customer Behavior Analysis

Key findings:

- **4,338 unique customers**
- **2,845 repeat customers (65.58%)**
- **1,493 one-time customers (34.42%)**
- **£2,048.69 average customer revenue**

## 🎯 RFM Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented using:

- **Recency** — How recently they purchased
- **Frequency** — How often they purchased
- **Monetary** — How much they spent
